ABOUT US

History

The roots of Sierra Leone's national power utility trace back to the National Power Authority (NPA), which was originally established by an Act of Parliament in 1982 (and previously known as the Sierra Leone Electricity Corporation).

Organization Structure

EDSA is led by a Board of Directors that oversees corporate governance and strategic policy. Executive management, headed by a Managing Director, directs specialized directorates responsible for commercial services, engineering operations, finance, and human resources.
