WELCOME TO EDSA

The Electricity Distribution and Supply Authority (EDSA) is mainly responsible for the supply, distribution, and retail sale of electricity in Sierra Leone.

Get Started

Recent progress reports over the years

  1. Meter Installation: Over 85% of connected grid customers now use prepaid meters, significantly improving revenue collection and billing accuracy.
  2. National Power Supply: Electricity access stands at ~30–35% nationally (~50% in urban centers like Freetown and under 5% in rural areas).
  3. Future Expansion Plans: Leveraging the $480M MCC Compact to upgrade Freetown's grid infrastructure.
  4. Regional Power Line: Utilizing the CLSG Regional Power Line to import reliable cross-border energy.
  5. Rural Mini-Grids: Expanding rural solar mini-grids to reach 78% national electricity access by 2030.
